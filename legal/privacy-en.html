Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Vocab Vault Operations respects your privacy. This policy describes how personal and learning data are collected, processed, and deleted.

1. Information Collected

  • Account Data: Email address, hashed authentication credentials, and session tokens.
  • Learning Data: Vocabulary entries, FSRS review schedules, notes, and sync watermarks across your 10 supported languages.
  • Billing Data: Payment card numbers are handled exclusively by Stripe, Inc. and never stored on our servers.

2. Purpose of Processing

  • Providing cloud synchronization, spaced-repetition scheduling, and AI etymology generation.
  • Managing subscriptions, preventing abuse, and enforcing rate limits.

3. Infrastructure & Subprocessors

  • Supabase, Inc. — Database hosting and authentication protected with TLS and Row Level Security.
  • Stripe, Inc. — Subscription billing and Customer Portal management.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini API) — Word and passage queries are transmitted without user email or identity fields.

4. Local Storage & Cookies

IndexedDB and localStorage are used strictly for offline-first persistence and user preferences. No third-party advertising trackers are used.

5. Permanent Account Deletion

You may permanently erase your account, cancel active Stripe subscriptions, and wipe all cloud and local records from Settings at any time.
