> Hey Product Hunt community! 👋

> Like many language learners and GRE/TOEFL aspirants, I spent years cycling through Anki decks, only to feel exhausted by isolated, context-free flashcards. Meanwhile, reading etymology books was fascinating, but without Spaced Repetition (SRS), I'd forget the insights within weeks.
